# Veyminore

**A technology venture in planning and development.**  
*Products · Experiments · Shared engineering systems*

[Repository](https://github.com/Z-A-R-R-O/Veyminore)

## Direction

Veyminore is the umbrella venture I’m building toward. The intention is to bring software products, experiments, reusable technology, engineering standards, and shared infrastructure into one coherent ecosystem rather than letting every idea become an isolated codebase.

Veyminore is not presented here as a launched company at scale. The work is in planning and development.

## Engineering workspace

The public repository describes a workspace organized around:

- Products for longer-lived product code and documentation.
- Labs for experiments and prototypes.
- Packages for reusable internal technology.
- Infrastructure for delivery, data, monitoring, and security configuration.
- Docs for architecture, decisions, and engineering standards.
- Templates and scripts for repeatable starting points and automation.

The root project uses pnpm and Turborepo commands for build, development, lint, test, typecheck, and cleanup workflows. The existence of these commands does not itself establish that every workspace passes them today.

## Product structure

ZETRO is documented in the workspace as a product direction with an executable adapter slice in development. **InterFrozt is another initiative under Veyminore**, focused on software products and startup-building; it is described separately in the [InterFrozt case study](interfrozt.md).

## What comes next

The next milestone is not a larger org chart. It is to make the boundaries, ownership, project status, and release expectations real through working products and repeatable engineering practices.

*Build the structure only as fast as the work can support it.*
