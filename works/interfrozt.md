# InterFrozt

**Software products and startup ideas under Veyminore.**  
*Initiative in development · Public site · Private source repository*

[Public site](https://interfrozt.vercel.app) · [Veyminore workspace](https://github.com/Z-A-R-R-O/Veyminore)

## Place in the ecosystem

InterFrozt is an initiative within Veyminore, not a separate company. Its intended direction is to be a home for software products and startup ideas—from initial concept through product engineering and digital experience.

**Veyminore is the umbrella. InterFrozt sits within it.**

## Current presentation

A public-facing site is available at [interfrozt.vercel.app](https://interfrozt.vercel.app). The source repository is private, so this case study links to the public experience rather than implying that the underlying implementation is open source.

## Current status

InterFrozt is part of a broader venture still being shaped. The public site is a front door, not proof that every intended product, service, or operating process is already live.

## Why it matters

The longer-term aim is to give software products and startup experiments a coherent home with a clear relationship to Veyminore, rather than creating disconnected brands without substance behind them.

*Let the structure support the product. Never the other way around.*
